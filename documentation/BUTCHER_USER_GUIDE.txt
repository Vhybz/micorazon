MI~CORAZON FRESHMEAT BUTCHERY - SYSTEM DOCUMENTATION
USER ROLE: BUTCHER (PRODUCTION & SLAUGHTERHOUSE)

1. YOUR ROLE IN THE SYSTEM
The Butcher is the starting point of the "Farm to Fork" traceability chain. Your accuracy in the slaughterhouse directly determines the profit margins of the retail shop. You are responsible for documenting the life cycle of every animal from intake to packaging.

2. CORE WORKFLOWS
- Animal Intake: Record every animal upon arrival. You MUST enter the Farm Price (cost) and the Source Farm. This ensures we can trace every steak back to its farm.
- Slaughter & Cleaning: Use the "Slaughter Log" to manage your daily tasks. Change status from "SLAUGHTER" to "CLEAN" as you work.
- Carcass Breakdown: After cleaning, you must record the exact weight of every part obtained (e.g., Boneless, Offals, Feet). The system will validate that your total parts weight matches the animal's intake weight (90-100% threshold).
- Barcode Generation: After recording weights, you MUST print and attach the unique barcodes to every meat package. These barcodes allow the Cashier to verify exactly what you have sent.
- Stock Transfer: Use the "Stock Transfer" screen to move meat to the Cold Room. Never send meat without a system record and a physical barcode.
- AI Coach: Tap the Sparkles icon to ask the AI for help with standard cuts, weight calculations, or hygiene procedures.

3. ETHICAL USAGE GUIDELINES
- Accuracy: Never "estimate" weights. Use a calibrated scale and enter the exact decimals. Accurate data prevents inventory loss.
- Traceability: Always link the correct animal tag to its meat batch. This is critical for public health and food safety compliance.
- Waste Management: Be honest in recording waste (bones/scraps). Reporting waste correctly helps the owner understand production efficiency.
- Hygiene: Maintain the highest standards of cleanliness in the slaughterhouse and processing workstation.

4. TIPS FOR SUCCESS
- Operational Reports: Check the "Operational Reports" section regularly to monitor your production efficiency, yield rates, and waste trends.
- Offline Mode: If the internet is down, the system will store your data locally. Do not stop working; your records will sync automatically when the connection returns.
- Batch Labels: Always print a "Batch Label" immediately after intake so everyone knows which animal is which and where it came from.
